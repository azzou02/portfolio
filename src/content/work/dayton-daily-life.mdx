<Section id="introduction" label="Introduction">

I designed the core screens of the Figma prototype for Dayton Daily Life, an all-in-one app that adds city guides, podcasts, transit, and a community forum to a local newspaper. Our team of four won the Cox Enterprise Case Competition among ~20 teams (Sept–Oct 2025). Judges said we clearly captured the market and the problem, connected our solution to Cox's other businesses, and landed on a podcast direction they were interested in.

<h3 class="cs-h2">What is Dayton Daily News?</h3>

The Dayton Daily News (DDN) is the daily newspaper of Dayton, Ohio, and part of Cox Enterprises' portfolio. It delivers print to most of Montgomery and Miami counties and parts of Greene County, and sells digital access and home delivery.

<ul class="cs-cards">
  <li class="cs-card"><h4>Today’s Product</h4><p>A website and ePaper, a daily Morning Briefing newsletter, and subscriber-only games and puzzles.</p></li>
  <li class="cs-card"><h4>Its Strength</h4><p>Our interviewees called it trustworthy, and one said it is the first result when she searches for Dayton news.</p></li>
  <li class="cs-card"><h4>Its Challenge</h4><p>Print readership is shrinking, the paper is ending its Saturday print edition, and it has little pull with younger readers.</p></li>
</ul>

</Section>

<Section id="problem" label="Problem" title="Grow a pro-print paper that is losing subscribers">

DDN is losing subscribers and engagement, as legacy print is. The client is pro-print, so dropping the paper was not an option. The brief asked us to size the market, propose a cost-effective growth strategy, and use Cox's other businesses.

  <li><b>02</b>Propose a cost-effective growth strategy</li>
  <li><b>03</b>Use Cox’s other businesses</li>
</ol>

<h3 class="cs-h2">My Role</h3>

I owned the Guides concept. In Figma I designed the onboarding flow (sign-in, login, register, interest selection) and the News, Guides, and Transit screens. A teammate designed Podcasts and Community. I also contributed to the interviews and helped with the market sizing.

</Section>

<Section id="key-decisions" label="Key decisions" title="Grow new readers, don't convert print readers">

Print is part of Dayton's identity, and the growth was in people DDN wasn't reaching. We targeted 25–40-year-olds with an all-in-one app that opens on familiar DDN news, then adds four tabs: Guides, Podcasts, Transit, and Community.

<Section id="transit" label="Design - Transit" title="Two tabs built on data that already exists">

Transit has two tabs. Traffic shows a live map and incident alerts. Transportation covers bike paths, bus schedule, walking routes, road closures, and cheapest gas. It relies on data that already exists from ODOT OHGO, GDRTA, and AAA, so it is buildable.

<Section id="reflection" label="Reflection" title="What I’d do differently">

<ol class="cs-list">
  <li><span class="n">01</span><div><h4>Rethink Transit</h4><p>Traffic came up often in interviews, but live traffic is a utility, not news. I would test whether commuters come back for it, and tie alerts to local reporting.</p></div></li>
  <li><span class="n">02</span><div><h4>Add a localized news view</h4><p>People value hyper-local coverage, so a page organized by neighborhood or city would strengthen the core product.</p></div></li>
  <li><span class="n">03</span><div><h4>Prototype the paywall and test with real users</h4><p>We did not prototype how Guides would be gated, and we did not test with 25–40-year-olds. I would do both before building.</p></div></li>
</ol>
